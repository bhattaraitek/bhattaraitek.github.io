Everything I know,  
Everything I believe,  
I made it up.

God or devil,  
Love or hatred,  
Success or failure,  
Joy or misery,  
Wonderful or dumb,  
I made it up.

Light or darkness,  
Truth or untruth,  
Life or death,  
I made it up.

What I failed to make?  
Nothing.  
All I know and believe,  
I made it up.

Warmth of the Sun, 
wonder of the Moon,  
The love of mother Earth,   
Caress of breezing wind and   
Soothing symphony of water,  
I made it up.  

In experiences of my life  
In the canvas of my mind.

If I disrobed me  
From what I made up     
An unknown void remains  
Which I do not know  
How to make it up?
