Imagine you're waiting for train at the station. You are full of stuffs with one 25kg, one 10 kg luggage, a backpack, and ukulele and a camera hanging around the neck. 
You have comfortably placed yourself on a bench nearby the platform, mostly busy with instagram, intermittently checking the clock for train.

Suddenly, you hear a alarm, and see some officials screaming *"Run.. Run..Run.. Go outside the station"* on top of his lungs. *"There's a gas leakage"*.

What will you do in this situation? Ideally, you will try to take all your stuffs and get out of the station. But, imagine everyone is running furiously, 
which makes you even more nervous since you get sense of some impending disaster. You also try to run but those weights are dragging you down, and there is staircase. 
You don't want to leave your stuffs there and you managed somehow halfway of stairs, while the officer is screaming at you leave it there, there's is gas leakage. 
Frightened and nervous you stumbled and your 25 kg luggage roll down the stairs. Finally, you decided to leave luggage and run away. 
Breathing heavily, pounding heartbeat, exhausted trying to run with big bag, you managed to escape the station. 

Few weeks ago, I encountered this situation at **Amsterdam Sloterdijk** train station on August 28, 2023. I was extremely nervous. After coming outside, under the anxious 
situation unable to figure out what's gonna happen, hopefully waiting for situation to get under control, a few things came up in mind. 
*"Why in the first place did I tried to run with all baggage? I could have leave them there and run. Are those stuffs more important than life?
The delay of few minutes could have had serious repercussion."* It was fortunate enough that the alarm was false, and nothing happened. 
But, the way I reacted to the initial evacuation instruction from officials was not rational. I learned valuable lessons that day and 
understood what to do during such emergency.

Here are the few things I realised that day:

- While packing things, keep all the important documents and accesories in the bagpack, 
which is easier to carry and does not hinder movement during the evacuation.

- Leave all the other baggage and things you need extra effort to carry at their position when there is alarm. 
Just run towards the exit. Do not waste energy and exhaust yourself on carrying those weights. No one will steal it. Even thieves love their life.
